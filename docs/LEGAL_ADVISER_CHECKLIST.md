# Checklist for Italian legal, notarial and accounting advisers

**Purpose.** A structured list of questions and deliverables for the lawyer (avvocato), notary
(notaio) and accountant (commercialista) advising the founders of The Blue Legacy Foundation.

**This is not legal or tax advice.** It was prepared to organise the conversation with qualified
professionals. Statutory references are indicative and must be verified by the advisers against
current law and practice (in particular the Codice del Terzo Settore, D.Lgs. 117/2017, its
implementing decrees, and the Civil Code).

Owner column: **L** = lawyer, **N** = notary, **A** = accountant, **F** = founders.

## 1. Legal form: Fondazione ETS vs Associazione ETS

| # | Question / deliverable | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Compare Fondazione ETS, Associazione ETS (riconosciuta / non riconosciuta) and alternatives for our model: family endowment + corporate sponsorship + events + grants to schools and sailing programmes | L, A |
| 1.2 | Minimum endowment (patrimonio minimo) and how it can be held; implications for legal personality and limited liability of directors | L, N |
| 1.3 | Which form best suits founder control vs independent governance and donor trust | L |
| 1.4 | Which "attività di interesse generale" (art. 5 CTS) cover school renovation, education, sport for disadvantaged youth and scholarships | L |
| 1.5 | Whether the gala and sponsorship are "attività diverse" (art. 6) or fundraising (art. 7), and the related limits | L, A |
| 1.6 | Alternatives if RUNTS registration is delayed or not chosen, and what we may then say publicly | L |

## Website items to change after advice

When the advisers confirm a point, update the corresponding text in `messages/en.json` and
`messages/it.json` (both!) and record the change in the audit trail of the website repository:

- Legal status labels (home "Launch status", Transparency "Legal status", banner, footer, terms)
- Privacy notice controller paragraph (`legal.controllerPending`)
- Donation page tax wording (`donate.tax.body`) and receipt notice (`donate.enabled.noTaxReceipt`)
- Partnership packages (`partners.packages`) — amounts and benefits
- Gala ticket information — only after permits and approval
