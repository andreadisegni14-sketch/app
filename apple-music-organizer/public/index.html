Apple Music Genre Organizer

Builds genre playlists from your library. Your existing playlists, favorites and library are only read, never changed.

1. Connect Apple Music

You'll sign in on Apple's own MusicKit page. This app never sees your Apple ID password, and you can revoke access any time in your Apple ID settings.

2. Analyze library

Reads all songs in your library, every playlist, and your Favorites. Nothing is written yet.
